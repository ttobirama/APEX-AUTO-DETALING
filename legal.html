Privacy, terms & accessibility

The legal information for APEX AUTO DETAILING — last updated August 2026.

Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains what we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Booking requests — name, contact details, vehicle type, service, and preferred date/time that you submit through the booking form.
  • Newsletter sign-ups — email address, if you choose to subscribe.
  • Usage data — standard web analytics such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to booking requests, confirm appointments, send the newsletter you asked for, and improve this website. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Cookies & third parties

This site uses Google Fonts and, in the contact section, an embedded Google Map — these may set cookies or make requests to third-party servers. If the booking form is connected to a backend (e.g. Formspree or a serverless endpoint), your submission is transmitted to that service for processing. Review their privacy policies for details.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at hello@apexauto.example. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Terms of service

These terms govern bookings and services provided by APEX AUTO DETAILING.

Quotes & estimates

All prices shown on this website are starting prices. The quote calculator provides an estimate only; the final price is confirmed after a vehicle inspection. We never begin work without a confirmed estimate.

Bookings & cancellation

Bookings may be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment. Late cancellations or missed appointments may be subject to a rescheduling fee.

Vehicle condition

We document the condition of every vehicle before work begins. Pre-existing damage, paint defects, or modifications are noted at drop-off and photographed where relevant. We use paint-safe techniques only, and take every reasonable care to protect your vehicle.

Limitation of liability

Our liability is limited to the cost of the service provided. We are not liable for defects in the vehicle itself, including pre-existing paint or coating failures, or for items left in the vehicle (please remove valuables).

Accessibility statement

We are committed to making this website usable by everyone, regardless of ability or technology. Our target is WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Full keyboard navigation — the gallery lightbox, FAQ accordion, and mobile menu are all operable by keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Screen-reader support — semantic landmarks, labelled controls, ARIA state on interactive widgets, and inline form error messages.
  • prefers-reduced-motion — all animations and transitions are disabled for users who request reduced motion.
  • Contrast & text — colour contrast meets AA thresholds and text remains resizable without loss of content.

Report an issue

Found something that doesn't work for you? We'd like to fix it. Contact us at hello@apexauto.example and we'll respond within 5 business days.
